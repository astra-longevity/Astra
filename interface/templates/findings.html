Mouse lifespan results (ITP)

The Interventions Testing Program feeds drugs to genetically mixed mice at three sites. Each dot is one group of treated mice (one drug, dose, start year and sex): how much longer, in percent, the middle mouse lived than in the untreated group. Green: the paper reports a lifespan gain. Grey: no clear effect. A hollow dot means some numbers in that file are still to be checked. Only the central value is shown: the files do not yet give uncertainty ranges, so no error bars are drawn. Click a dot to open its file.
