# VO2max (maximal aerobic capacity)

## What it measures

The most oxygen the body can use per minute during all-out exercise, usually in mL per kg body weight per minute. Measured by breathing through a mask on a treadmill or bike while effort rises to exhaustion (cardiopulmonary exercise test, CPET). The best single number for heart–lung–muscle fitness.

## Reference population

- Age norms: 3,816 healthy Norwegian men and women aged 20–90 (HUNT fitness study, Loe 2013), treadmill, direct gas analysis. Their equipment's test-retest correlation was 0.99, CV 1.8% (lab check on staff, not on participants).
- Day-to-day noise: 742 healthy adults tested on two days (HERITAGE study, re-analysed by Shephard 2004).

## Noise

| Study | Population | ICC | Day-to-day CV / error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shephard 2004 (HERITAGE re-analysis) | 742 healthy adults, 2 days | — | CV 5.0% before training, 4.1% after |
| Edgett 2018 | Young recreationally active adults, 3 tests each | 0.938–0.992 | CV 4.9% |
| Succi 2023 | Physically active women, 2 tests | 0.984 | CV 1.98%, SEM 0.77 mL/kg/min, minimal difference 2.14 mL/kg/min |
| Beltz 2016 (review, citing Vickers and Katch) | Meta-analysis and repeated testing | — | Mean standard error 2.58 mL/kg/min; total error ±5.6%, of which ~93% biological, ~7% measurement |

- In plain English: one person's VO2max moves about **2–5% between days** with no real change (table above). In Succi 2023, a change smaller than 2.14 mL/kg/min was within noise for one person.
- Most of that noise is **the body, not the machine**: day-to-day biology dominates (Beltz 2016).
- Shephard's point: with a 5% day-to-day CV, much of the apparent "non-response" to training in group studies is just test-retest error. Same applies to the president's readings.

## Known failure modes

- **Not reaching true maximum** (effort-limited stop). Rule: a verification bout after the ramp test. Labs use different agreement thresholds to confirm a true maximum (Costa 2021, meta-analysis); the threshold must be fixed in advance.
- **Estimated VO2max is not VO2max.** Sub-maximal or formula-based estimates (step tests, cycle formulas, wearables) are not direct measures. Only a direct gas-analysis CPET counts as this input.
- Treadmill gives higher values than bike; mode must be fixed.
- Heat raises day-to-day error; test in a fixed, cool room.

## Functional anchor and age trajectory

Itself a functional test. Declines with age across 20–90 in Loe 2013.

## Confounders

Training status (changes in weeks), body weight (per-kg value moves with weight), sex, altitude, heat, sleep, recent illness, beta-blockers. List not exhaustive.
