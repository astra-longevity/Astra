# Cognitive test (choice: NIH Toolbox Cognition Battery)

## Choice

T3-A2-1 asks for one cognitive test with published test-retest data in healthy adults of the president's age. Candidates read so far:

| Battery | Retest data found | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NIH Toolbox Cognition Battery (NIHTB-CB)** | Adults, composite and per-test ICCs, published reliable-change cut-offs (Heaton 2014; Karr 2024) | **Chosen**: best documented, published cut-offs, about 30 min on a tablet |
| CANTAB | 75 healthy adults, 3 months apart: acceptable reliability on only 3 of 14 outcome measures; practice effects on 6 (Karlsen 2020) | Too noisy for single-person tracking |
| Cogstate | Built for repeat testing; practice effect removed by a familiarisation run (Falleti 2006, ages 18–40); no checked retest figure for 40–60 year-olds yet | Backup; data on 40–60 year-olds thin |

Karr 2024 retest sample: 138 adults aged 18–85 (mean 54.8, SD 20.0; 51% men), retested about one week apart. This covers the president's age.

## What it measures

Computer tests of memory, attention, switching tasks, working memory and processing speed. They combine into a **Fluid Cognition Composite** (abilities that decline with age) and a Crystallized Composite (vocabulary, reading, which hold up with age). For ageing, the Fluid composite is the input.

## Noise

| Score | ICC / r | Reliable change (80% confidence) | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fluid Cognition Composite, age-adjusted standard score | ICC 0.87 | change ≥ 10 points | Karr 2024 |
| Fluid Cognition Composite, demographic-adjusted T-score | ICC 0.84 | cut-off published in Karr 2024 but cut off in the copy read here; use the standard-score row | Karr 2024 |
| Five fluid tests, one by one | ICC 0.64–0.86 | 8–19 points depending on test | Karr 2024 |
| Composites (adults) | r 0.86–0.92 | — | Heaton 2014 |

- Retest interval in Karr 2024 is about one week; longer intervals were not tested there.
- In plain English: on the Fluid composite (mean 100, SD 15 for standard scores), a change below **about 10 points (two-thirds of an SD)** is noise. Single tests are noisier still.
- Practice effects are small to medium (Cohen's d 0.09–0.52) and must be subtracted (Karr 2024).
- Looking at many tests at once inflates false alarms: in healthy people retested, 32% "declined" on at least one test, but only ~10% on two or more (Karr 2024, T-scores, 90% CI, practice-adjusted). Rule: **judge change on the composite**, or require two tests to move.

## Known failure modes

- Practice effect between sessions 1 and 2 (do a familiarisation session first).
- Sleep, mood, stress and time of day move scores.
- Normed scores depend on the norm sample; use raw or the same norm version throughout.

## Functional anchor and age trajectory

Fluid cognition is itself the function. Fluid composite falls with age in the NIH Toolbox norming data (Heaton 2014). No slope per year is quoted here; read it from the norming tables before use.

## Blind spot

Brain ageing beyond these domains (e.g. structural change) is not covered by one battery; listed as partial cover until axes.md is aligned.
