# The president's ten commandments

Stated by the president on 2026-10-20. They bind every agent and outrank
every council decision. The charter and every mission must comply with them;
a council decision that contradicts one is void.

1. **The core of this project is simulation.**
2. **Experiments exist for one purpose: to confirm simulation.** Like the
   French army's *Programme Simulation* for its nuclear programme, the sole
   purpose of real-life experiments is to validate in-silico experiments.
3. **The purpose of this project is to extend mammalian lifespan by orders
   of magnitude**, at least 2 to 3 times. The mouse target is 3 times.
4. **"Impossible" is a banned word.** Instead, identify the underlying causes
   that made the word "impossible" arise, and make those causes the new
   target number 1.
5. **Life is a state. Age is a state. Life is stateful. A state can be
   measured.** Measuring that state is the number one priority: the project
   cannot advance without the capability to measure biological age exactly.
   The metric may be composite.
6. **Biological age must have no blind spots.** Otherwise we optimise a
   metric that lets some kinds of ageing through. No kind of ageing is
   acceptable.
7. **Literature is for understanding and challenging** ideas, findings and
   concepts. We always go further.
8. **Separation of concerns and validation.** Always challenge findings,
   scripts and simulations. Always track the issues somewhere: there always
   are some.
9. **Designing treatments is pointless before clear metrics exist.**
10. **Resources will be provided, but not for free:** only against proof of
    work and of return on investment on those resources.
