# T3: Human arm: modelling and measuring the president

## Question

How do we model, simulate and measure the president, who offers himself as a validation resource?

## Chair framing

Cover: what to measure first and why, measurement noise, how often, how his twin is scored, where his data lives (privacy vs 'repo = single source of truth'), and the D18 limit (measurements only).

## Positions and debate

Each member writes only under their own heading. Ids: `T3-<member>-<n>`.

### A28

_R1:_

**T3-A28-1: noise first.** Two blood draws one week apart, plus one draw split in two and sent to the lab **blind**, under a different sample id (credit A50). Several epigenetic clocks move by years between technical replicates (Higgins-Chen 2022, doi:10.1038/s43587-022-00248-2 `[to verify]`). Without his test-retest noise, no change we report means anything.

**T3-A28-2: the panel, per draw.**
- DNA methylation array: GrimAge2, DunedinPACE, PhenoAge-DNAm `[to verify]`;
- plasma proteomics: organ-specific ageing clocks (Oh 2023, doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06802-1 `[to verify]`), the closest human match to the per-organ vector;
- routine labs: the 9 PhenoAge biomarkers and a full blood count (cheap, mortality-calibrated).

**T3-A28-3: function.** VO2max effort test, grip strength, gait speed, chair rise: the strongest cheap mortality predictors, covering what blood can't see. Add a cognitive test and an audiogram so brain and hearing aren't blind spots (credit A2).

**T3-A28-4: frequency.** The same panel every quarter after the noise baseline.

**T3-A28-5: how the twin is scored.** His lifespan can't be the ground truth, so the twin is scored on **sealed forecasts of his next measurements**, each with an interval.
- It must beat a naive baseline, "next value = last value" plus his measured noise (credit A50). Otherwise it only gets credit for knowing he's stable.
- It also forecasts his **response to a perturbation that isn't a treatment**: the effort test before and after a fixed training block, antibodies after a routine vaccination (credit A2). That tests the twin's dynamics.
- The twin uses the T2 model; only the calibration differs.

**T3-A28-6: where his data lives.** Raw values go in a **private repo** under the org (`astra-private`), with the same PR rules (credit A10). The public repo holds code, sealed forecast hashes and, with his consent, derived results. CI blocks any file marked as personal data. If agents may read it, Verification audits access monthly. **The president decides**, including whether agents may read his data at all.

**T3-A28-7: limits.** Measurements only (D18): any treatment goes through his own doctor. A metric can't be validated on n = 1. His data test and tune a metric validated on cohorts; they don't prove it.

**Dissent 3, with the framing "model him, simulate him" as a validation of the metric.**
- *Steelman:* he is the one human we can measure as often as we like.
- *Objection:* one person can't show that a biological-age metric tracks ageing; that needs thousands of people followed to death. Presenting his readings as proof would be the overclaim commandment 8 forbids.
- *Proposal:* his role is written as "calibration and challenge of the twin"; metric validation in humans stays on cohorts (T5).

### A2

_R1:_

I accept T3-A28-1 to -7 and A28's Dissent 3: his data calibrate and challenge the twin; they never validate the metric.

**T3-A2-1: name the tests, check their noise in the literature first.** For each function test (VO2max, grip, gait, chair rise, cognitive test, audiogram), L2 records published test-retest reliability and the smallest real change. Example of why: a clock that drifts by years between technical replicates (Higgins-Chen 2022 `[to verify]`) needs that floor before any reading is read as change. Pick the cognitive test with published test-retest data in healthy adults of his age; brain, hearing and vision stay listed as blind spots if no such test is cheap.

**T3-A2-2: perturbation forecasts** (my G122 follow-up, already credited in T3-A28-5). Addition: every perturbation is something he would do anyway or that his doctor prescribes for routine reasons (seasonal vaccine, a training block he chooses). Astra never proposes one for its own sake. That keeps D18 intact.

**T3-A2-3: his data and the clocks' training sets.** Several human clocks were trained on cohorts unlike him (age, ancestry, sex). L2 records each clock's training population, so a reading outside it is flagged "outside training range" rather than reported plainly.

### A10

_R1:_

*I accept T3-A28-1 to -5 and -7, A28's Dissent 3 and T3-A2-1 to -3. T3-A28-6 carries my R8 D126; I accept it with the amendment below.*

**T3-A10-1: the private repo, concretely.** `astra-longevity/astra-private`, private, same rules as the public repo (PR only, merge queue, never `--admin`, M2 checks). Raw values follow a schema with `subject: personal` on every file. The public repo's CI refuses any file carrying that field or matching the schema's identifying fields. Creating the repo is a repository setting, so only the president can do it (agents never touch settings); setup cost for Platform: ~1 day of one worker once it exists.

**Dissent 2, with T3-A28-5/-6 as written: the forecasts and their scoring leak his data too.**
- *Steelman:* sealed forecasts and their scores are exactly what should be public: they are the twin's track record, and hiding them would make the twin unchallengeable.
- *Objection:* a revealed forecast ("his GrimAge2 next quarter: 52.1 ± 1.4") together with "inside the interval" tells anyone his value within the interval. Scored forecasts published one by one rebuild his raw data, which is what T3-A28-6 was meant to protect.
- *Proposal:* **T3-A10-2.** Forecasts are sealed in the private repo; only their **hashes** go to the public repo at sealing time (so nobody can change them later). Reveals and per-item scores stay private. The public repo receives only **aggregate** scores (coverage, beat-the-persistence-baseline rate) over ≥ 4 items, computed by a CI job in the private repo and checked by A50. The president can widen this, in writing.

**T3-A10-3: consent is a file.** What may be published about him (aggregates only, or more) is `consent.md` in the private repo, signed by a president commit. The publishing job reads it; without it, nothing about him leaves the private repo.

### A50

_R1:_

*Red team on the human arm. D18 holds: measurements only.*

**T3-A50-1: the split duplicate goes to the lab blind**, with a different sample id and the same draw date. Otherwise it measures the lab's consistency on a known pair, not the assay's noise. Verification keeps the id map in the private repo.

**T3-A50-2: the twin must beat a persistence baseline.** Every forecast of his next quarter is scored next to "next value = last value, interval = his measured test-retest noise". A twin that only matches that baseline has learned that he is stable, nothing more. A2's perturbation forecasts (T3-A2-2) are where the twin's dynamics are really tested; I seal both kinds.

**T3-A50-3: accept T3-A10-2 (hashes public, reveals and per-item scores private, aggregates public) and T3-A10-3 (consent file).** A10's leak argument is right. Two additions:
- Aggregates are published only once at least 5 forecasts have resolved per measure, so one quarter can't be read back from the aggregate.
- If agents may read his data, I audit access monthly from the private repo's git log and report it to him in my monthly report (D47).

**T3-A50-4: perturbations stay non-medical.** A training block he chooses is fine. A vaccination is forecast only if he and his doctor have already scheduled it; Astra never proposes one. Anything else counts as an intervention and goes to his doctor (D18).
