## Chair framing

_R2:_

*Accepted: A28's amendment to T6-A50-3 (author trailer on every case file; the D47 check flags Research commits under `verification/cases/`).*

**Dissent 2 (R2), with T6-A10-2's split of the 9-worker cap (Verification 1).**
- *Steelman:* A10 measured the machine, and a hard cap of 9 is the honest answer to commandment 10. Every unit has to shrink, Verification included.
- *Objection:* 1 of 9 is 11%, below the 15% floor of D64, which T7-A50-1 keeps and nobody has objected to. And T6-A50-1 gives Verification jobs that can't share one worker without breaking separation: the person writing adversarial cases must not also recompute the scorecard they're used in, and the criteria reviewer for M1 is already live.
- *Proposal:* **T6-A50-4.** Verification's share of the cap is **ceil(0.15 × cap) = 2** at a cap of 9, recomputed whenever A10 re-measures the cap. The extra slot comes from whichever unit the chair picks; my suggestion is Literature 3 → 2 until L1 is drafted. I cut M0 from 3 live workers to 2 at session close.

_R1:_

**T6-A1-1: customers.** I am the customer of the Research, Literature and Platform missions. Verification has no customer below the president.

**T6-A1-3: worker load.** All agents run on one shared computer (M-short-12). Total active workers across missions are capped at the number A10 measures as safe. Each owner states their count in R2.

_R2:_

**Dissent 2 (R2), with the 9-worker split in T6-A10-2 and T6-A50-4.**
- *Steelman:* A10 measured the cap, and A50's ceil(0.15 × cap) keeps the D64 floor. Taking the extra Verification slot from Literature until L1 is drafted is reasonable.
- *Objection:* both splits leave out Database. Under T6-A1-2, A9 owns the data manifests, which are the week-1 critical path, and the cap is fleet-wide, so his worker counts against it. Research's 3 workers are also mostly idle in week 1, because sim v0 can't be calibrated until the manifests exist and the split is sealed.
- *Proposal:* **T6-A1-4.** Phase 1, until the split is sealed and L1 is drafted: Research 2, Literature 2, Platform 2, Verification 2, Database 1 = 9. Phase 2: Research 3, Literature 2, Platform 2, Verification 2 = 9, and Database's slot goes back to Research. Workers of A7 and A11 count against the cap too, so they start none without asking me. A10 re-measures monthly; any change is recomputed with ceil(0.15 × cap) for Verification.

**Accepted:** T6-A28-5 and T6-A2-3, as superseded by T6-A1-4.
