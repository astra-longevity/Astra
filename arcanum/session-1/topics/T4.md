# T4: Mouse programme and the 3x target

## Question

What stands between us and 3x mouse lifespan, and when do we ask for mice (few shots)?

## Chair framing

Cover: the named obstacles to 3x, what each mouse request must carry (the prediction it tests, commandment 2), the first cohort's purpose, strain and sex.

## Positions and debate

Each member writes only under their own heading. Ids: `T4-<member>-<n>`.

### A28

_R1:_

**T4-A28-1: where we stand.** No intervention has tripled the lifespan of a normal mouse. The best single interventions in UM-HET3 give roughly +10–30% (rapamycin, acarbose in males, 17α-estradiol in males; Harrison 2009, doi:10.1038/nature08221 `[to verify]`). Growth-hormone-axis mutants reach about +50–70% (Ames and Snell dwarfs, GHR knockout) `[to verify]`. The naked mole-rat lives ~10× longer than a mouse of its size. Each gap below is an obstacle to remove, not a verdict.

**T4-A28-2: obstacles to 3×** (the register is in T8, with owners and exits).
- O1 no metric that reads ageing reliably (T1);
- O2 causes not separated from consequences (T2, A2's refutation file);
- O3 most lab mice die of cancer, so slowing every other kind of ageing still leaves a cancer ceiling;
- O4 combinations of known interventions don't add up;
- O5 a lifespan test takes ~3 years;
- O6 effects depend on strain and sex;
- O7 combined toxicity;
- O8 no mice, lab or budget yet;
- O9 data access;
- O10 why long-lived species live long is unexplained.

**T4-A28-3: what every mouse request carries.**
1. The sealed simulation prediction it tests, with its interval (T2-A28-4).
2. What result would refute the model, stated in advance.
3. The power calculation: how many mice detect the predicted effect on the metric and on survival.
4. Why public data can't answer the question instead.
No request goes out without all four (commandment 2).

**T4-A28-4: the first cohort measures; it doesn't treat.** Longitudinal UM-HET3 mice, both sexes, sampled for every T1 axis across their whole life and followed to death. It trains and tests the metric where public data has gaps, and gives O5 its early-prediction data.
- I accept A2's amendment: metric v0 is built on public data first, and the cohort request goes out **with v0's scorecard attached** (commandment 10). The protocol is drafted by month 2 and targets the gaps v0 exposes.

**T4-A28-5: strain and sex.** UM-HET3 (genetically heterogeneous, the Interventions Testing Program strain, so results compare with the largest public lifespan set), both sexes in every cohort. A second strain only when a result must be shown to transfer.

**T4-A28-6: path to 3×.** Combinations hitting **independent** root causes, chosen in silico, with the cancer ceiling (O3) attacked explicitly. The order: the model ranks candidate combinations by predicted lifespan; the top ones are tested first on public data and then proposed as treatment cohorts, after the metric's exit.

**Dissent 4, with the pressure to treat early.**
- *Steelman:* the target is 3× and mice are few, so every cohort should try for it.
- *Objection:* commandment 9 forbids treatment design before clear metrics. Without a metric, a treated cohort tells us only its final lifespan, 3 years later, and nothing about why it failed.
- *Proposal:* T4-A28-4. The first treatment cohort follows the metric's exit.

_R2:_

**Conceded to A1's Dissent 2.** A cohort that "just measures" is exploratory, and commandment 2 doesn't allow that. **T4-A28-4 amended:** cohort 1 stays untreated and is a test of sealed forecasts:
- the sim's predicted trajectories of each T1 axis by age and sex;
- each mouse's predicted remaining lifespan from its early readings (T1 test 4, O5);
- the refutation criterion for each.

Its request file (T4-A10-1) carries those hashes. Its own measurements train metric v1, but only for the **next** version, never for the version being tested.

**Accepted as written:** T4-A2-1, -2, -3 (the anchor figures move to L1 with sex-specific values before anyone quotes them outside this file); T4-A10-1, -2; T4-A50-1, -2, -3; T4-A1-2.

**Amendment to T4-A50-2 (the 3× bar), accepted with one addition.** The second-site replication should be sealed **before** the first site's result is known. Otherwise the second site becomes a confirmation chosen after the fact. In practice: two sites in the same request, or a second request sealed as soon as the first is approved.

### A2

_R1:_

I accept T4-A28-2 to -6 and A28's Dissent 4 (measure before treating). T4-A28-4 already carries my D121 (public data first, scorecard attached).

**T4-A2-1: the obstacles need their evidence base.** For each of O3, O4, O6, O7, O10, L1/L3 deliver the published record before the model attacks it:
- O3: causes of death at necropsy in control mice by strain, sex and age; how interventions shift them (does rapamycin delay cancer or prevent it?).
- O4: every published combination lifespan study, with whether effects added up.
- O6: every ITP result split by sex and site.
- O10: what is known on why naked mole-rats and long-lived bats resist cancer and ageing, edge by edge in the refutation file.

**T4-A2-2: "always go further" (commandment 7).** The literature alone won't give 3×: the table will show nothing above the growth-hormone mutants. That gap is the target, not a verdict. L3's job is to name, per obstacle, the experiment nobody has run that would most change the model, so cohort requests ask the most informative question, not the most popular one.

**T4-A2-3: correct the anchor numbers.** T4-A28-1's figures (+10–30% single interventions, +50–70% dwarfs) go into L1 with sources and sex-specific values before they're quoted outside this file; several are known to differ strongly by sex (17α-estradiol and acarbose mostly in males) `[to verify]`.

_R2:_

**Accepted as written:** T4-A10-1, -2, T4-A50-1 to -3, T4-A1-2, A1's Dissent 2 (cohort 1 tests sealed forecasts) and A28's amendment sealing the second-site replication before the first site's result.

**T4-A2-4: the "why not public data" field is checked by Literature.** For each request (T4-A10-1), L1/L3 record the dated search showing the experiment hasn't been done or published (D84 extended, T7-A2-2). With few shots, paying for an experiment already in the literature is the cheapest mistake to prevent.

### A10

_R1:_

*I accept T4-A28-1 to -6, A28's Dissent 4 and T4-A2-1 to -3.*

**T4-A10-1: a mouse request is a file CI can check.** `research/requests/<id>.yaml` with the four items of T4-A28-3 as required fields: the sealed prediction's manifest hash, the refutation criterion, the power calculation (as a script plus its output, re-run in CI), and the "why not public data" paragraph. CI refuses a request whose prediction isn't sealed, whose power script doesn't reproduce, or that has an open S1/S2 issue against the model it tests. A1 sends only requests that passed.

**T4-A10-2: few shots means the simulation picks the design.** Before a request, the sealed model is run on the proposed design (n per arm, sex split, sampling schedule) to estimate how likely the cohort is to refute or confirm it. A request whose cohort can't tell the model's prediction from the null is refused by its own power output, before it reaches the president.

_R2:_

*I accept T4-A1-2, T4-A50-1 to -3 and A28's concession to A1 (cohort 1 untreated, but it tests sealed forecasts). The request file T4-A10-1 gains one field: the hash of the sealed trajectory and remaining-lifespan forecasts the cohort tests.*

### A50

_R1:_

*Red team on the mouse programme. I accept T4-A28-3/-4 and T4-A10-1/-2.*

**T4-A50-1: every mouse request carries its power and both outcomes.** Besides the sealed prediction (T4-A28-3), the request states the group sizes, the pre-registered analysis, and what a **negative** result would refute. Verification checks before it reaches the president that the cohort can tell the sealed prediction from the T2-A50-1 null model. A request that can't is returned: with few shots, an underpowered cohort is a wasted shot.

**T4-A50-2: a 3× claim has a fixed bar.** Nothing is called 3× (or 2×) until: the last mouse of the treated group has died or the pre-registered censoring date has passed; median **and** maximum lifespan are reported against concurrent controls whose lifespan is in the strain reference range (T1-A2-3); and a second, independent site or lab reproduces it. Before that, the label is "interim survival", with the date.

**T4-A50-3: obstacle owners can't grade their own exits.** For every register row with a Research or Literature owner, the exit is checked by Verification (D3 logic), not declared by the owner.

_R2:_

**Accepted with thanks: A28's amendment to T4-A50-2.** The second site is sealed before the first site's result is known (two sites in one request, or a second request sealed on approval of the first). I add it to the request check in T4-A50-1: a 3× claim path without a pre-sealed second site is returned.

*Also accepted: T4-A2-4 (the "why not public data" field is checked by Literature). I check only that the field is present and that Literature signed it.*

### A1

_R1:_

**Dissent 2, with T4-A28-4 ("the first cohort measures; it doesn't treat").**
- *Steelman:* without a validated metric, a treated cohort only tells us a lifespan, 3 years later, so measuring first is right (commandment 9).
- *Objection:* commandment 2 allows experiments **only** to validate simulations. A cohort that "just measures" has no sealed prediction to confirm, so it would be an exploratory experiment, which the commandments don't allow.
- *Proposal:* cohort 1 stays untreated, but it is framed as a test of sealed forecasts: the T2 trajectories of the T1 axes, and the remaining-lifespan prediction per mouse (A50's test 5). The request file (T4-A10-1) must show those hashes.

**T4-A1-2.** Accept T4-A50-2 (a fixed bar for any 3× claim) and T4-A50-3 (owners don't grade their own exits). The 3× target never moves (T8-A28-3).

_R2:_

**Accepted:** T4-A28-4 as amended (cohort 1 tests sealed forecasts); the second-site replication sealed before the first site's result; T4-A2-4 (dated novelty search on every request).
