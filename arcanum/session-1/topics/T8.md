### A2

_R1:_

I accept T8-A28-1 (register, never moving an exit after the fact), T8-A28-2 and T8-A28-3.

**Dissent 3, with O2's exit "≥ 80% of test-set effects in direction".**
- *Steelman:* direction is the first thing a causal model must get right, and 80% is demanding for biology.
- *Objection:* published mouse interventions are mostly reported **extensions** (publication bias, and ITP picks promising candidates). A model that predicts "extends lifespan" for everything may pass 80% on direction while understanding nothing.
- *Proposal:* **T8-A2-1.** O2's exit is scored against a sealed "always extends" baseline and a "same as the closest training intervention" baseline; the model must beat both, and the test set must include the ITP null arms and lifespan-shortening controls (T1-A2-4).

**T8-A2-2: Literature obstacles.** Added to the register, owner A2:
| # | Obstacle | Exit | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| O11 | Published lifespan effects are biased (sick controls, unpublished nulls) | L1 complete with T1-A2-3 flags; share of flagged results reported | month 3 |
| O12 | Causal edges asserted without refutation tests | Every sim v0 edge has an L3 row with its refuting experiment | before sim v0 calibration |

**T8-A2-3: Literature asks for no resources** beyond its four workers. Proof of work for later requests: L1's audited extraction error rate.
