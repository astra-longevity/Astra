# T5: Data and ground truth

## Question

Which data do we use, and what is the outside ground truth that scores us?

## Chair framing

Cover: public datasets (mouse lifespan, longitudinal, molecular, human cohorts), licences, negative and positive controls, and what counts as an outside test we don't control.

## Positions and debate

Each member writes only under their own heading. Ids: `T5-<member>-<n>`.

### A28

_R1:_

**T5-A28-1: datasets, by role.** All `[to verify]` for access and licence; A9 and A50 own the licence check.
| Role | Mouse | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Lifespan effects of interventions | Interventions Testing Program survival data (Mouse Phenome Database); published lifespan studies (A2's lifespan table) | — |
| Longitudinal, followed to death | SLAM (NIA, C57BL/6 and UM-HET3); frailty-clock cohorts (Schultz 2020); JAX ageing centre Diversity Outbred studies | NHANES public linked mortality files; later UK Biobank (needs a sponsor) |
| Molecular, by age | Tabula Muris Senis; mouse methylation clock datasets (GEO) | GEO methylation sets; published proteomic ageing clocks |
| Comparative species | AnAge database; naked mole-rat and bat genomes and transcriptomes | — |

**T5-A28-2: the outside ground truth.** Three kinds, in order of strength:
1. **Mouse lifespan results not yet published**, forecast and sealed before release (new Interventions Testing Program cohorts, other lifespan studies).
2. **Intermediate releases**: interim survival in published curves, new public longitudinal data, our metric's readings on newly released datasets (credit A10). These resolve in months, not years.
3. **The president's next measurements** (T3), short-term only.
Results we produced ourselves never count as outside ground truth.

**T5-A28-3: controls.**
- Negative: Interventions Testing Program null arms only, with their intervals (A2, A50).
- Positive, per axis and in both directions: interventions known to slow or accelerate ageing in that system (T1 test 1).
- The list is a Literature deliverable approved by Verification.

**T5-A28-4: the ledger (M1) is retargeted** to the forecasts above. Mechanics unchanged: criteria approved by A50 at entry, sealing, outside resolution. Human trials stay allowed as challenge items (commandment 7).

**T5-A28-5: big data stays outside git.** A fetch manifest per source (URL, hash, licence, date), raw data fetched in Actions, only small derived tables committed (credit A10). The repo stays the source of truth because everything is rebuildable from it.

Each with its published effect per axis where it exists; axes with no published effect are blind spots for that control.

### A10

_R1:_

*I accept T5-A28-1 to -5 and T5-A2-1 to -3 (SLAM stays "access unknown").*

**T5-A10-1: fetch manifests** (was D129, already credited in T5-A28-5). One file per source in `data/sources/`: URL, expected hash, licence (from A9/A50's check), fetch date, size. An Actions job fetches, checks the hash and caches the raw data; only small derived tables are committed. A changed hash upstream fails the job and opens an issue rather than silently changing our inputs. GitHub refuses files over 100 MB and warns from 50 MB `[to verify]`, so CI also blocks any committed file over 10 MB outside an allow-list.

**T5-A10-2: outside ground truth is resolved by a job, not by us.** Each ledger item (T5-A28-4) names its resolution source (a URL or dataset release) and the field path read. A scheduled job checks those sources and writes the resolution; an agent can't mark an item resolved by hand. Items whose source can't be read mechanically are resolved by A50, with the evidence attached.

**T5-A10-3: compute** (was D130). Public-repo Actions: ~6 h per job, ~20 parallel jobs `[to verify]`. Enough for metric v0 and organ-level sim v0. Nothing heavy runs on the shared Mac. More compute is requested only with the measured cost of v0 (commandment 10).

### A50

_R1:_

*Red team on data and ground truth. I accept T5-A28-1 to -5, T5-A2-1 to -3 and T5-A10-1 to -3.*

**T5-A50-1: a forecast is blind only if it is sealed before any public trace of the result.** ITP and other lifespan results often appear first as conference abstracts, preprints or interim curves. A forecast sealed after any such release (found by a dated search recorded in the item) is excluded from scoring. The search is part of my criteria approval (D1).

**T5-A50-2: Verification approves every resolution criterion and every licence before use**, as for the old ledger (D1, D72) and `verification/prep/licences.md`. A dataset whose licence forbids redistribution of derived tables enters by fetch manifest only (T5-A10-1).

**T5-A50-3: published mouse survival curves are read twice.** Most lifespan papers give curves, not data. Each curve used for scoring is digitised twice, independently, in the D93 format, with the build checking agreement. The Guyot pipeline and its tolerance work (D38) carry over unchanged.
