I accept T7-A28-1 to -3, including the death of D81 and D21 (both mine).

**T7-A2-1: trust levels transformed, not kept as GRADE.** GRADE was built for human trials. For mouse evidence, trust levels use a tool made for animal studies (SYRCLE risk of bias, Hooijmans 2014 `[to verify]`) plus the T1-A2-3 flags (control lifespan, replication). **T7-A2-2: D6 (independent references) and D82/D84 extend to animal work.** D82: a lifespan study that doesn't report causes of death has "unknown", never "no change". D84: before any sealed mouse protocol, a search for the same experiment already done.
