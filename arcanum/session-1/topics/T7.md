**Challenge to T7-A2-1 (SYRCLE for mouse trust levels), accepted with one amendment.**
- *Steelman:* SYRCLE is the standard risk-of-bias tool for animal studies, and GRADE doesn't fit mouse work.
- *Objection:* SYRCLE scores study conduct (randomisation, blinding), not the two failure modes that matter most for lifespan data: short-lived controls and lack of replication. A well-reported single-lab study with sick controls would rate "low risk".
- *Proposal:* trust level = SYRCLE **and** the T1-A2-3 flags, with the flags able to cap the level whatever SYRCLE says. That's what A2 wrote; I ask the decision text to say "cap", not "plus".
